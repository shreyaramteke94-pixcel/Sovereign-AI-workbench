 POL-ACC-003
Access Control Policy
Identity, authentication, authorization, and access-review requirements for organizational systems.
Document Owner
Identity and Access Management
Classification
Internal — Enterprise Policy
Version
1.0
Effective Date
01 September 2026
Review Cycle
Annual or after major access-control changes
Key Rule
Organizational credentials belong to the assigned user. Never share passwords, authentication codes, or
access tokens with another person.
1. Purpose
 Ensure access to organizational systems and information is granted only to authorized users.
 Apply least-privilege and need-to-know principles to reduce unauthorized access.
2. Account Management
 Each employee must use their individually assigned organizational account.
 Users must not share organizational usernames, passwords, authentication codes, or access tokens.
 Accounts must be disabled or adjusted when employment status or role changes require it.
 Privileged or administrative access must be separately controlled and granted only when required.
3. Authentication Requirements
 Users must protect passwords and authentication credentials from disclosure.
 Passwords must not be written in publicly accessible locations or shared with other people.
 Multi-factor authentication must be used where the organization has enabled or requires it.
 Authentication codes, recovery codes, and security tokens must be treated as confidential credentials.
4. Authorization and Least Privilege
 Access must be limited to systems and information required for an employee’s assigned responsibilities.
 Requests for additional access must follow the organization’s approval process.
 Access to Confidential and Restricted information requires appropriate authorization.
 Users must not attempt to bypass access controls or use another person’s account.
5. Access Reviews
 Access rights should be reviewed periodically by responsible system or data owners.
 Unnecessary or outdated access must be removed promptly.

 Privileged access requires additional review and monitoring.
6. Remote and External Access
 Remote access to internal systems must use organization-approved secure access mechanisms.
 Users must not bypass organizational access controls using unauthorized remote-access tools.
 External service providers may receive access only through approved authorization and security procedures.
7. Reporting
 Suspected credential compromise, unauthorized access, or account misuse must be reported to the IT security
team as soon as possible.
 Users must cooperate with security actions such as credential resets, access reviews, or account restrictions
when required.
