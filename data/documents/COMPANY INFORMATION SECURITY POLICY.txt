COMPANY INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY 
1. Password Security 
 
Employees must use strong passwords for all company accounts. Passwords must not 
be shared with other employees. 
 
2. Company Devices 
 
Employees must use organization-approved devices when accessing confidential 
company information. 
 
3. Data Protection 
 
Confidential company information must not be copied to personal cloud storage or 
personal devices. 
 
4. Security Incidents 
 
Employees must report suspected security incidents to the IT security team as soon as 
possible. 
 
5. External Sharing 
 
Confidential company documents must not be shared with external individuals or 
organizations without authorization from the department head.
