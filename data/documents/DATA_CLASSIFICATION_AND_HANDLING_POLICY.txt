 POL-DATA-001
Data Classification and Handling Policy
Classification, storage, transmission, and sharing requirements for organizational information.
Document Owner
Information Security Office
Classification
Internal — Enterprise Policy
Version
1.0
Effective Date
01 September 2026
Review Cycle
Annual or after a material security change
Key Rule
Confidential and Restricted information must remain within organization-approved systems unless an
authorized exception or approved external-sharing process applies.
1. Purpose
 Define a consistent classification system for organizational information.
 Specify appropriate storage, access, transmission, and sharing requirements.
 Reduce the risk of unauthorized disclosure, loss, or misuse of organizational data.
2. Data Classification Levels
 Public — Information approved for unrestricted public distribution, such as published company announcements
and approved marketing material.
 Internal — Routine business information intended for employees and authorized contractors. It must not be
distributed publicly without approval.
 Confidential — Sensitive business, employee, customer, financial, technical, or operational information
requiring controlled access.
 Restricted — Highly sensitive information requiring the strongest access controls. Access is limited to
specifically authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis.
3. Handling Requirements
 Employees must store Confidential and Restricted information only in organization-approved systems.
 Confidential and Restricted information must not be stored on personal computers, personal phones, or
personal cloud-storage accounts.
 Employees must verify recipients before sending Confidential or Restricted information.
 External sharing of Confidential information requires authorization from the responsible department head or
designated data owner.
 Restricted information must not be externally shared unless explicitly authorized by the designated data owner
and required controls are in place.
 Printed Confidential or Restricted material must be protected from unauthorized viewing and securely disposed
of when no longer required.

4. Transmission and Sharing
 Use organization-approved secure channels when transmitting Confidential or Restricted information.
 Do not upload organizational Confidential or Restricted information to unapproved online services.
 Do not forward sensitive organizational information to personal email accounts.
 When external sharing is approved, share only the minimum information required for the business purpose.
5. Employee Responsibilities
 Classify information before storing or sharing it.
 Follow access restrictions associated with the classification level.
 Report suspected loss, accidental disclosure, or unauthorized access to the IT security team as soon as
possible.
 When uncertain about classification or handling, consult the Information Security Office or data owner.
6. Policy Exceptions
 Exceptions require documented approval from the appropriate data owner and Information Security Office.
 Approved exceptions must define the scope, duration, business justification, and required safeguards.
