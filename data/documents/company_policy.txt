COMPANY REMOTE WORK POLICY 
 
Employees may work remotely for up to three days per week 
with prior approval from their department manager. 
 
Employees must remain available during official working hours 
from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. 
 
Employees working remotely must use the organization's 
approved VPN when accessing internal systems. 
 
Confidential company information must not be stored on 
personal devices. 
 
Remote employees must follow all organizational security 
and data protection policies. 
 
For extended remote work beyond three days per week, 
approval from the department head is required.
